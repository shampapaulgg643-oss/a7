Editorial Policy

The standards behind every guide on Field Coat Meteor.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

We want readers to trust what they find here. These standards guide how we research, write and update every page.

Independence

We accept no payment, free gear or affiliate arrangements in exchange for coverage, and we keep our guides brand-neutral. If advertising appears on the site, advertisers have no influence over content.

Research and accuracy

Our guides draw on textile and outdoor-industry references, published testing methods for waterproofing and breathability, and practical experience in a range of weather. We avoid overstating what any fabric or feature can do, and we flag where performance depends on the specific product.

Safety first

Where advice touches on outdoor safety, we write conservatively and encourage readers to check forecasts and local guidance.

Corrections

Spotted a mistake? Tell us through our contact page. We check every report, fix confirmed errors quickly and update the page date when changes are significant.

Images and tools

Photographs are sourced from Unsplash under the Unsplash License and described with accurate alternative text. Interactive tools such as the weather picker are created by us and presented as general guidance.
