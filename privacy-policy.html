Privacy Policy

How Field Coat Meteor collects, uses and protects personal information.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Field Coat Meteor (“we”, “us”, “our”) publishes www.fieldcoatmeteor.com, a free guide to outdoor jackets and outerwear care. We aim to collect as little personal information as possible. This policy explains what we collect and how it is used.

What you share with us

When you send a message through our contact form, we receive your name, email address, the topic you choose and your message. When you sign up for Field Dispatch, we receive your email address. We use these only to reply to you or to send the seasonal email. We never ask for payment details, as nothing is sold on this website.

What is collected automatically

Like most websites, our server and analytics tools record technical details such as your IP address, device and browser type, operating system, the page that referred you, the pages you view and when. The weather picker on our homepage runs in your browser and does not send your selections to us. More detail is in our Cookie Policy.

Why we use information

  • To answer your questions and corrections.
  • To deliver the newsletter you requested.
  • To learn which guides are helpful and improve the website.
  • To protect the website from spam, abuse and security threats.
  • To comply with applicable law.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics helps us understand visitor numbers and behaviour. It uses cookies to collect information such as pages viewed and session length, which Google processes under its own terms; see How Google uses information from sites that use its services. The Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on lets you prevent this collection.

Advertising

We may show advertisements provided by Google and other third-party vendors. These vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info/choices.

Sharing

We never sell or rent personal information. It is shared only with providers that help run the website, such as our host and email service, or when disclosure is legally required.

How long we keep it

Messages are kept only as long as needed to respond and follow up. Newsletter addresses are stored until you unsubscribe. Analytics data follows the retention period set in our Google Analytics account.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR and CCPA, you may be entitled to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information. Contact us to make a request. Every newsletter contains an unsubscribe link.

Children

Our website is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children and will delete it if we discover we have.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including HTTPS, to protect information. No online service can be completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Updates

We may revise this policy from time to time; the date above shows when it last changed.
