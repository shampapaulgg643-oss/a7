Outdoor conditions

Clothing advice cannot replace preparation and judgement. Weather in mountains, coastal areas and remote places can change rapidly. Always check local forecasts and safety guidance, and carry appropriate equipment for your activity.

Ratings and comparisons

Fabric bars, waterproof and breathability ranges and the weather picker are simplified guides. Real performance depends on construction, fit, age, care and conditions. Always refer to the manufacturer’s specifications.

Care advice

Garments vary, and the care label on your jacket always takes priority. We are not responsible for damage resulting from cleaning, re-proofing, re-waxing or repair methods described on this website.

Meteor shower information

Shower dates are approximate and visibility depends on location, weather, moonlight and light pollution. Consult an up-to-date astronomy source before planning a night out.
