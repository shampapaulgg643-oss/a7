Cookie Policy

How Field Coat Meteor uses cookies and how you can control them.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy explains the cookies and similar technologies used on www.fieldcoatmeteor.com. Please read it together with our Privacy Policy.

About cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. They let the site remember your choices and help its owners understand how it is used. Some are set by us and some by third-party services we rely on.

Types we use

Essential

We store your answer to our cookie notice in your browser’s local storage so we do not ask again on every page. This is required for the website to behave as expected.

Analytics

Google Analytics uses cookies such as _ga and _ga_* to count visits and see which guides are read, in aggregate. These usually expire after up to two years.

Advertising

If ads are shown, Google and other vendors may set cookies to personalise ads based on your visits to this and other websites, and to measure ad performance. Control personalised ads at Google Ads Settings.

Controlling cookies

Choose “Accept” or “Essential only” in our cookie notice, or manage cookies at any time in your browser. Blocking some cookies may affect features of the site.

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Changes

We may update this policy as our tools or legal obligations change. The date above shows the current version.
