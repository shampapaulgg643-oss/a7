Our story

About Field Coat Meteor

A small, independent guide for people who spend time outdoors and want clear, honest advice on the jackets that keep them comfortable.

How it started

A cold night and a long list of questions

The idea for this website came on a cold August night, lying in a field to watch the Perseids in a jacket that was perfect for the walk there and hopeless for hours of sitting still. The next morning we started reading about insulation, fabrics and layering, and we have not really stopped since.

Field Coat Meteor brings that research together. The name reflects the two things we love most: practical outerwear for the field and a good excuse to be outside after dark.

Independent

We do not sell jackets, accept sponsored placements or promote particular brands.

Practical

Our guides focus on real conditions and real use, not marketing language or trends.

Made to last

We encourage buying thoughtfully, caring well and repairing before replacing.

How we research

Tested in everyday weather

Our advice combines textile references, information on industry testing standards and plenty of time spent outdoors in wind, rain and cold. When information is general or varies by product, we say so clearly.

You can read more about our standards in our Editorial Policy.
